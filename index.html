Pick a song

This is a fanchant project. izna's songs are here block by block, and nayas record the shouts one at a time. Every voice sent in is edited together into one crowd for the finished video.

Before you start

  1. Go somewhere quiet, and keep the mic close to your mouth.
  2. Press Guide to hear it, then Record and sing along.
  3. Redo anything as many times as you like.
  4. After you finish, upload your takes.

! By taking part, you agree to your voice being used in a public fanchant video. All recordings are deleted once the project is finished.

Guides

A clip for this block, or the whole song to place every block from. Then drag the line onto the first word. Whatever runs up to it is played under the count; where there is none, the count is silent.
